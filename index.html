The Golden Grove 50-Point Cleaning Standard

Our systematic cleaning checklist ensures nothing is missed. Every room is thoroughly sanitized using hospital-grade, pet-safe products and HEPA-filtered equipment.

Kitchen Sanitation

  • Disinfect & polish countertops & backsplashes
  • Scrub & sanitize sink, faucet & drain area
  • Clean exterior of all appliances (fridge, oven, dishwasher)
  • Microwave interior & exterior deep cleaning
  • Wipe exterior cabinets, tables & chairs
  • Vacuum & sanitize mop kitchen flooring

Bathroom Disinfection

  • Deep scrub & sanitize shower, tile & bathtub
  • Disinfect toilet inside, outside & behind base
  • Clean & shine vanity countertops & sinks
  • Streak-free mirror polishing & chrome fixture shine
  • Color-coded microfiber to prevent cross-contamination
  • Mop tile flooring with antibacterial cleaner

Living & Bedrooms

  • Dust all reachable furniture, shelves & décor
  • Wipe light switches, door handles & baseboards
  • HEPA filtration vacuuming of carpets & rugs
  • Dust ceiling fans, light fixtures & window sills
  • Hardwood & laminate floor sweeping and damp mopping
  • Empty all trash receptacles and replace liners
